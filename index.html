Protecting the Accuracy of the Nation’s Measurements

The Measurement Integrity Division ensures commercial, industrial, and scientific measuring devices meet federal standards for precision and fairness.

About the Division

Established in 1973, the Federal Office of Standards and Metrics (FOSAM) is responsible for maintaining the integrity of weights, measures, and calibration systems used in interstate commerce and public safety applications. The Measurement Integrity Division conducts field inspections, device certifications, and investigations into potential measurement discrepancies.

Always verify the primary standard before secondary instruments. Never assume factory calibration is current. Document every adjustment. Inspect the zero point carefully. Review historical drift data. Every device tells a story if you know how to read it.

Core Functions

Field Calibration

On-site verification and adjustment of commercial scales, meters, and dimensional tools.

Integrity Investigations

Examination of reported or suspected inaccuracies in measuring equipment used in trade or regulation.

Standards Compliance

Certification that devices conform to national measurement standards.

Technical Support

Guidance to state and local weights-and-measures authorities.
